# Tekmetric Training Entrypoints

Primary public training sections:

- Repair Order Workflow: https://support.tekmetric.com/hc/en-us/sections/20506623273879-RO-Creation-Estimate-Building-Training
- RO Creation & Customers: https://support.tekmetric.com/hc/en-us/sections/360005929393-RO-Creation-Customers
- Shop Management: https://support.tekmetric.com/hc/en-us/sections/20507454952471-Shop-Management

Training scope visible from public sources:

- RO creation.
- Customer management on repair orders.
- Estimate building.
- Inspection workflow.
- Performing work in Work-In-Progress.
- Payments.
- Refunds and part returns.
- Internal notes.
- GP/hr goals.

Operational takeaway: Tekmetric training is organized around repair-order lifecycle work, not generic modules. ARK docs and UI should follow that same lifecycle-first mental model.
