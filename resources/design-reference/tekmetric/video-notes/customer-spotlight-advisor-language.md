# Tekmetric Customer Spotlight: Advisor Language

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7OdDHEeqN2E

## Workflow Patterns

- The service advisor describes the system as a way to answer customer concerns, bundle the information, and keep customer/technician communication together.
- Inspection report and estimate are separate but sequential customer-facing artifacts.
- Advisor and technician notes are described as a shared communication loop, not detached comments.

## Pacing Observations

- Customer-facing flow is: answer concerns -> send inspection report -> customer visually understands issue -> send estimate -> customer sees why and how much -> advisor/technician notes keep the work moving.
- The emotional posture is convenience and reduced time spent chasing context.
- Operational value comes from bundling related information in one place.

## Review/Edit Posture

- The advisor frames the estimate as explanatory: what is quoted, why it is quoted, and how much it is.
- Technician notes support advisor review and customer communication.
- The estimate should read as a clear explanation, not just a set of editable line items.

## Language

- Service adviser
- Customer concerns
- Bundle everything up
- Inspection report
- Visually see what we're seeing
- Estimates
- What we're quoting
- Why we're quoting it
- Technician notes

## ARK Implications

- ARK estimate review should explain work in operational/customer language, not generic item management language.
- Notes should support workflow continuity between technician and advisor.
- Customer concern, inspection evidence, estimate lines, and approval posture should feel bundled.
