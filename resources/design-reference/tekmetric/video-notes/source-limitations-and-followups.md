## Limitation

The most useful Tekmetric operational training material appears in gated demos, support articles, or webinar experiences that are not fully downloadable as public video assets in this environment. Public YouTube results skew toward customer spotlights, integrations, and short feature clips.

## Useful Patterns Still Confirmed

- Job Board is the daily operational command center.
- Repair orders move through Estimates, Work-in-Progress, and Completed.
- Approval state drives queue movement.
- Advisor review bridges technician findings and customer-facing estimates.
- Mobile supports check-in, RO lookup, DVI, photo/video/markup, and time/work progress.

## Followup Worth Doing

If a logged-in demo, support portal, or screen recording is available later, prioritize capturing:

- full estimate tab mutation flow
- dense customer-facing authorization screen
- RO summary tab with authorization history
- customer profile and job history
- Tech Board assignment changes
- mobile check-in from VIN/plate scan through RO creation
