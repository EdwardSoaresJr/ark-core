# AutoLeap Operational Lifecycles

## Core Work Board Model

AutoLeap centers daily shop flow around a visual Work Board.

- Estimates and repair orders appear in one operational window.
- Jobs move across stages with drag-and-drop.
- Technicians can be assigned or reassigned from the board.
- The board exposes ticket numbers, customer information, payment status, inspection status, pending work, and technician availability.
- Overdue jobs can trigger alerts.
- Technician filters support workload balancing.

ARK implication: queue screens should help the advisor see work, ownership, payment, inspection, and delay pressure in one scan.

## Estimate To Work Order Lifecycle

AutoLeap’s public workflow describes:

1. Create customer profile and vehicle data.
2. Add services using canned jobs, labor guides, parts lookup, or DVI recommendations.
3. Attach inspection reports, notes, photos, and videos.
4. Send estimate by text or email.
5. Customer approves or defers services digitally.
6. Approved estimate converts into work order with one click.
7. Work order is assigned to technicians.
8. Completed estimate/work order converts to invoice with one click.

Operational pattern: estimates, inspections, work orders, invoices, and customer history are framed as one connected workflow.

## Approval And Deferral

AutoLeap emphasizes digital customer approvals:

- Estimates are sent by SMS or email.
- Customers approve or defer recommended services.
- DVI evidence is attached to increase approval confidence.
- Deferred services are tracked and resurfaced later through CRM/follow-up.
- Approval rates, advisor performance, deferred work, and profit by job are treated as management metrics.

ARK implication: deferral is not failure. It is future service inventory and should remain visible in customer/vehicle context.

## DVI Lifecycle

AutoLeap DVI flow:

1. Attach a pre-built inspection checklist when creating an estimate.
2. Technician performs inspection on phone/tablet.
3. Inspection uses color-coded status and canned notes.
4. Technician attaches photos, videos, audio, notes, and annotations.
5. Technician recommends services.
6. Customer receives report and can approve or defer.

ARK implication: inspection findings should feed estimate building directly, not sit in a separate media gallery.

## Work Order Execution

AutoLeap presents work orders as clear technician assignments:

- Approved estimates become detailed work orders.
- Tasks are assigned to technicians.
- Technicians receive instant notifications through the Technician App.
- Job progress updates in real time.
- Advisors and managers track progress from the Work Board.

ARK implication: production status should be advisor-visible without requiring a separate technician-management screen.
