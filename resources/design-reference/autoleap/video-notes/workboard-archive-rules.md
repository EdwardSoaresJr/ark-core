# AutoLeap Workboard: Archive Rules

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tHsfyKEYyjE

## Workflow Patterns

- The workboard is framed as the first surface many shops look at when starting the day.
- Queue posture is operationally scoped: keep visible work focused on cars in the shop, invoices, payment state, and jobs still needing attention.
- Archive rules are not decorative cleanup; they are a workflow control that prevents paid/finished work from polluting daily scanability.
- The interaction happens from lane-level controls, which keeps workflow management close to the queue rather than hidden in global settings.

## Pacing Observations

- Advisor/owner movement starts with scanning the board, then narrowing to a lane, then using a small contextual action.
- The video emphasizes reducing overwhelm in busy shops by keeping the workboard relevant.
- The rhythm is: glance, identify stale/paid state, open lane menu, set automatic cleanup.

## Language

- Workboard
- Starting your day
- Status
- Paid column
- Archive when paid
- Cars in the shop
- Waiting to get paid

## ARK Implications

- ARK queues should avoid becoming permanent historical archives.
- Completed/paid/closed states should remain retrievable, but daily queues should privilege current work and actionability.
- Queue controls should be near the workflow they affect, not buried in generic admin settings.
