## Limitation

Public AutoLeap videos expose useful workflow sequencing, especially workboard and end-to-end estimate-to-invoice flow. Dense, logged-in customer profile and approval review screens remain less available publicly.

## Useful Patterns Confirmed

- Workboard is treated as the start-of-day operational surface.
- Archive rules keep the queue focused on active work and unpaid/pending states.
- Estimate -> DVI -> parts/inventory -> digital approval -> invoice -> payment is the core continuity path.
- Technician app supports assigned tasks, clock-in, inspection updates, and real-time office communication.
- Reporting appears as a post-workflow management surface rather than the primary repair-order workspace.

## Followup Worth Doing

If a logged-in demo or longer onboarding recording is available later, prioritize capturing:

- dense estimate review with approved/declined mix
- customer profile and service history
- fleet customer workflow
- technician app inspection checklist in motion
- approval screen from the customer perspective
- workboard with many mixed statuses and assignments
