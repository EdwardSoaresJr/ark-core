# AutoLeap Step-by-Step Shop Workflow

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7zZZRb-VMOc

## Workflow Sequence

- Customer call or appointment starts the job.
- Estimate is created before work execution, with recommended services, labor, and pricing presented transparently.
- Digital vehicle inspection attaches photos/videos directly to the estimate context.
- Parts and inventory are handled without leaving the workflow.
- Customer approves digitally.
- Approved estimate becomes an invoice without retyping.
- Payment processing and reporting close the loop.

## Pacing Observations

- The operational story is linear and repetitive: appointment -> estimate -> DVI -> parts -> approval -> invoice -> payment -> reporting.
- The system is positioned around continuity: no re-entry, no missing charges, no leaving the operational surface for parts/inventory.
- Reporting appears after execution, not as the primary daily work surface.

## Review/Edit Posture

- Estimate review is customer-facing and trust-oriented.
- Editing happens before approval: add services, labor, parts, inspection evidence.
- After approval, the posture changes from estimate mutation to invoice/closeout.

## Language

- Estimate
- Recommended services
- Labor and pricing
- Digital vehicle inspection / DVI
- Parts and inventory
- Digital approval
- Invoice
- Payment processing
- Real-time insights

## ARK Implications

- ARK should preserve this workflow continuity: estimate lines, inspection evidence, approval, invoice/closeout should feel like one operational path.
- Estimate mutation should be intentional and upstream of approval.
- Reporting should support decisions but not displace the repair-order workflow as the center of gravity.
