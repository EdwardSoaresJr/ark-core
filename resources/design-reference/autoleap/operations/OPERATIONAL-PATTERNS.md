# AutoLeap Operational Patterns

## Reporting Posture

AutoLeap frames reporting as real-time management visibility, not decorative analytics.

Public pages prioritize:

- Sales.
- Labor costs.
- Parts margins.
- Profit margins.
- Technician productivity.
- Attendance.
- Billable hours.
- Estimated versus actual hours.
- Discounts, labor, and fees.
- Payment summaries.
- Advisor performance.
- Approval rates.
- Deferred work.
- Profit by job and service.

ARK implication: reporting should answer management questions quickly and use shop vocabulary.

## Management Questions

AutoLeap repeatedly frames management around:

- What is profitable?
- Which technician is productive?
- Which jobs are delayed?
- Which services generate margin?
- Which advisors close fastest?
- Which deferred services can be recovered?
- Where are payment and invoice states?
- What workload is assigned to each technician?

ARK implication: KPI surfaces should be grounded in decisions, not chart variety.

## Technician Flow

Technician App pattern:

1. Technician logs in on phone or tablet.
2. Technician sees assigned tasks and schedules.
3. Technician clocks in for the day.
4. Technician logs time against individual services.
5. Technician conducts inspections.
6. Technician updates job progress.
7. Technician flags issues.
8. Office receives real-time status changes.

ARK implication: technician workflow should be task-oriented, service-lane friendly, and tightly connected to advisor visibility.

## Customer And Vehicle Flow

AutoLeap emphasizes connected customer history:

- Customer communication lives in the same operational system as estimates and invoices.
- Service reminders and follow-ups re-engage customers.
- Deferred services are tracked for future approval.
- Fleet customers centralize repair orders, invoices, vehicle histories, preferences, and custom pricing.
- Vehicle history and service records should be instantly available.

ARK implication: customer/vehicle records should support service-lane continuity and future revenue recovery.

## Fleet And Multi-Location Pattern

AutoLeap fleet workflow emphasizes:

- Fleet customer profile.
- Multiple vehicle histories.
- Custom pricing and labor rates.
- Advance planning of service orders.
- Technician and bay assignment.
- Real-time job progress.
- Bulk billing/invoice posture.
- Cross-location visibility in higher tiers.

ARK implication: fleet support should not require a separate product mental model; it is customer/vehicle grouping plus pricing, scheduling, and reporting discipline.

## Financial Authority

AutoLeap financial posture prioritizes:

- Profitability reports.
- Labor, parts, tires, and other margin breakdowns.
- Sales, orders, and payment trends.
- Invoice/payment tracking.
- QuickBooks/accounting integration.
- One-click estimate-to-invoice conversion.

ARK implication: financial truth should remain close to workflow state and should not become a detached BI surface.
