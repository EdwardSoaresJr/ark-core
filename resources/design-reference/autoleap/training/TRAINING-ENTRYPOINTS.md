Training scope visible from public sources:

- Work board management.
- Estimate creation and digital approval.
- DVI-to-estimate workflow.
- Work order assignment.
- Technician app usage.
- Time tracking.
- Reporting and profitability review.
- Customer communication and deferred service follow-up.
- Fleet management.

Operational takeaway: AutoLeap messaging is built around one connected system: estimate, DVI, approval, work order, technician execution, invoice, payment, follow-up, and reporting.
