# Margin levers

Product must reinforce these levers in reporting and workflow. Numbers come from posted/closed sales truth unless labeled otherwise.

## 1. Posted labor rate and ELR

- Raise posted rate in small, consistent steps.
- Track **effective labor rate** = labor dollars ÷ billed hours on posted work.
- Free inspections, underpriced menus, and unpaid diag dilute ELR.

**In ARK:** Operational Report → Effective Labor Rate; Owner Targets → ELR floor.

## 2. Parts matrix

- Margin comes from system pricing, not advisor discounting.
- Target ~55–58% parts margin on known costs.
- Review matrix as average part cost rises; simulate closed samples before changing live policy.

**In ARK:** Parts Margin KPI; Owner → Parts Matrix Tune.

## 3. Inspection → ARO

- Write up needed work; let the customer decide.
- Deferred scopes are follow-up revenue when disposition is honest.

**In ARK:** ARO on Executive Pulse; Deferred Opportunity; advisor inspection guides.

## 4. Tech → advisor workflow

- Concise findings and complete parts lists keep advisors from chasing techs.
- Technician efficiency on the Production tab.

## 5. Mix and net

- Balanced posted mix often near ~55% labor / ~45% parts — set shop targets in Settings.
- Net profit targets belong on real P&L; ARK Owner P&L is a management estimate only.
