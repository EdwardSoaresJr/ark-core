## Desktop catches up to phone

When operator opens desktop after phone-first work, authority should already reflect:

- Customer arrived and checked in
- VIN scanned, vehicle verified
- Concern and photos captured
- Technician assigned
- Inspection progress and findings
- Customer replied; advisor responded
- Payment taken; RO closed

Desktop is **secondary execution** — not the source of operational truth during the floor day.
