## Why Google says "not top 3 yet" (hypothesis)

Google does not have emotions. It has **signals**:

1. **Review volume + velocity** — incumbents have 3×–20× reviews; gap compounds monthly if capture rate is low  
2. **GBP engagement** — photos, posts, services, Q&A (mostly unmeasured for competitors today)  
3. **Time** — competitors have 10–50 years of consistent Maps presence; digital authority cannot be compressed into weeks  
4. **Organic depth** — symptom pages building, but index age is ~36 days  
5. **Citations / backlinks** — unknown; likely favors long-tenured shops  

**Reviews alone are not 80% of the gap.** Time + combined local signals are. But **review velocity is the highest-leverage signal LugsNPlugs controls this month.**
