# ARK trademarks and branding

**Project policy — not legal advice.**

Copyright protects ARK’s **code**. This file covers the **name and brand**.

## What you may do

Under the AGPL-3.0-only license (see `LICENSE`), you may use, copy, modify,
fork, and redistribute ARK’s covered software — including commercially —
subject to that license’s conditions (including AGPL §13 for network use of
modified versions).

You may name your public fork something else and describe it accurately as
based on or derived from ARK.

## What you may not do

Do **not** use the **ARK** name, logos, or related branding in a way that
implies your modified or hosted product is the **official ARK distribution**,
or that it is endorsed by Edward Soares Jr. or the ARK project, without
permission.

Examples that are not OK without permission:

- Presenting a fork as “the ARK project” or “official ARK”
- Using ARK logos or wordmarks as the primary brand of a competing hosted
  product in a way that confuses operators about origin
- Claiming independent authorship of Edward Soares Jr.’s copyrighted ARK code

Forking under AGPL and calling the fork a distinct product name is generally
fine when notices and attribution required by the license are preserved.
